💡Some key business insights from the analysis:

🔹 Offline-Agent channel generated the highest revenue and customer share

🔹 Customers aged 31-40 formed the largest customer segment

🔹 Senior customers aged 51+ showed comparatively higher settlement risk

🔹 Delhi NCR emerged as the top-performing city in revenue and customer contribution
